Membership

Join a community of accredited investors making a difference in Central Illinois.

Why Join

Invest Alongside Experienced Angels

Central Illinois Angels provides accredited investors with access to curated deal flow, shared due diligence, and a collaborative investment community. Our members benefit from collective expertise and reduced individual risk through group investing.

Members participate in monthly meetings, exclusive networking events, and have the opportunity to mentor the next generation of entrepreneurs in our region.
