Join a community of accredited investors dedicated to fostering innovation and economic growth in Central Illinois.

Why Join CIA?

Why Join CIA?

As a member of Central Illinois Angels, you gain access to vetted investment opportunities you wouldn't see otherwise. We streamline the process of finding, screening, and investing in high-growth startups.

Request Information

Membership Benefits

  • Exclusive access to high-quality deal flow
  • Monthly screening meetings and presentations
  • Collaboration with experienced investors
  • Due diligence support and templates
  • Networking events with founders and peers
  • Educational resources on angel investing
  • Access to Angel Capital Association benefits

Membership Requirements

Accredited Investor

Net worth over $1 million (excluding primary residence) OR annual income exceeding $200,000 ($300,000 joint) for the past two years.

Active Participation

Attend monthly membership meetings and/or participate in various committees.

Investment Champion

Willingness to serve as an investment champion for potential portfolio companies.

Financial Commitment

Annual membership fee of $1,000. Members are encouraged to invest $50,000 in the first three years.

Interested in Joining?

Visit our contact page and indicate your interest in becoming a member. Our Membership Committee will review your application.
