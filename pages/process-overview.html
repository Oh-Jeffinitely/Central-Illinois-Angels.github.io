How It Works

Our Investment Process

A transparent, structured approach to evaluating and supporting promising ventures.

Step by Step

From Application to Investment

Our process is designed to be thorough yet efficient, giving every applicant a fair evaluation.

1

Initial Application

Submit your application through our online form. Include your business plan, financial projections, and executive summary. We accept applications on a rolling basis throughout the year.

2

Preliminary Review

Our screening committee reviews your application within two weeks. We evaluate market opportunity, team capabilities, competitive landscape, and alignment with our investment criteria.

3

Presentation

Selected companies are invited to present to our full membership. This 20-minute presentation followed by Q&A gives our investors a chance to learn about your vision and ask questions.

4

Due Diligence

A dedicated team of members conducts thorough due diligence, reviewing financials, technology, market validation, legal standing, and management team background.

5

Investment Decision

Our members vote on the investment opportunity. If approved, we work with you to finalize terms and structure the deal in a way that benefits both parties.

6

Post-Investment Support

Our relationship doesn't end at funding. We provide ongoing mentorship, board participation, strategic introductions, and support through future growth stages.
