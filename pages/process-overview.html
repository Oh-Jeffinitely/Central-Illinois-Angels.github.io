How It Works

Process Overview

Understand our investment process from application to funding and beyond.

1
Week 1

Submit Application

Complete your application through our Gust portal with your pitch deck, executive summary, and financial projections.

2
Weeks 2-3

Initial Screening

Our screening committee reviews all applications and selects companies that align with our investment criteria.

3
Week 4-6

Pitch Presentation

Selected companies are invited to present to our full membership at our monthly meeting.

4
Weeks 6-10

Due Diligence

Interested members form a due diligence team to conduct a thorough evaluation of your company.

5
Week 10-12

Member Vote

The due diligence team presents findings to membership, followed by an investment vote.

6
Week 12+

Investment & Partnership

Upon approval, we finalize terms and close the investment. Our relationship continues as we support your growth.

What to Expect

Our process typically takes 10-12 weeks from initial application to investment decision. We've designed it to be thorough yet efficient, respecting both your time and ours.

Throughout the process, you'll have the opportunity to engage with experienced investors who can provide valuable feedback regardless of the final investment decision.

Companies that receive funding become part of our portfolio family. We provide ongoing support, make introductions, and help you navigate the challenges of scaling your business.
