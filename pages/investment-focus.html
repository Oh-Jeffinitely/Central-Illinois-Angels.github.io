Our Approach

Our Investment Strategy

Central Illinois Angels focuses on early-stage companies that demonstrate strong growth potential, capable leadership teams, and a clear path to market. We invest in businesses that align with our regional strengths and where our members can add meaningful value beyond capital.

Our investments typically range from $50,000 to $500,000 per deal, with the ability to participate in follow-on rounds as companies scale. We take a collaborative approach, working closely with founders to maximize their chances of success.
