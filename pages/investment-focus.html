Industry Focus

Industry Focus

Currently, Central Illinois Angels does not have a specific industry focus. We have a very diverse membership that includes individuals with backgrounds in a number of industries and experience in numerous management levels and company stages.

Investment Stage

Central Illinois Angels does not have a specific investment stage requirement. As is the case with most angel groups, we typically review seed and early stage (Series A) investments, but the stage of the investment is not a limiting factor.

Typically, businesses seeking investment should have an identified and qualified lead investor.

Type of Entity

Central Illinois Angels prefers to invest in C-Corporations. This structure provides the most flexibility for equity investments and future funding rounds.

Investment Range

We typically invest between $50,000 and $500,000 per company, often participating in larger funding rounds alongside other investors.
