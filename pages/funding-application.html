Central Illinois Angels invests in early-stage companies with strong growth potential. Our application process is designed to be straightforward and transparent.

We review applications on a rolling basis and aim to provide initial feedback within two weeks of submission. Our team evaluates each opportunity based on market potential, team strength, and alignment with our investment focus.

What We Look For

Investment Criteria

Understanding our criteria will help you prepare a strong application.

📈 Revenue Requirements

We typically invest in companies with demonstrated revenue or strong evidence of market traction. Pre-revenue companies with exceptional technology or IP may also be considered.

🏭 Industry Focus

Technology, healthcare, manufacturing, agriculture, clean energy, and education are our primary sectors, though we consider compelling opportunities across all industries.

🌎 Geographic Focus

We prioritize companies headquartered in or with significant operations in Central Illinois. Companies with strong ties to the region are also considered.

🚀 Growth Stage

Seed to Series A stage companies are our sweet spot. We invest between $50K and $500K per deal, with the ability to participate in follow-on rounds.
