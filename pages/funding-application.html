Funding Application

Take the first step toward securing investment for your startup.

In order to be considered for funding, please submit an inquiry through our Contact Us page. You will then need to provide several pieces of information about your company and the funding that you desire.

Do not include confidential information. Neither Central Illinois Angels nor its members sign Non-Disclosure Agreements for initial proposals. If there is sufficient interest, a very specific NDA may be signed at a later time during the due diligence process.

For specific details on our investment process, please refer to the Process Overview page.

Before you apply, please be aware that angel funding is a very specific type of equity funding for startup and rapidly expanding businesses. Keep in mind, that like most angel investment groups, Central Illinois Angels is looking for companies that present the opportunity for significant growth and an appropriate pre-money valuation. In general, angel investors are looking for companies that can provide at least a 10X return in a seven-year period.

What We Are Looking For In An Investment Opportunity

1

Sufficient Market Opportunity

Is the market for the product or service large enough to justify the financial projections and growth as well as the investment?

2

Strategy to Capture/Retain Market Share

Once the market opportunity is shown to be sufficient, how will you be able to capture your piece of the market? What channels and sales strategies will you use? How will competitors respond to your entry and to your product?

3

Barriers to Market Entry

Is there intellectual property associated with your product or idea? What will keep competitors from eroding your market share?

4

Strong Management

Does the current management team have the necessary experience to execute the full business plan? If not, how will the appropriate management be added?

5

Exit Strategy in Place

How will the investors be able to profitably exit the business in 3 to 5 years?

6

Seek Advice/Coaching

Are you willing to get advice and coaching from investors?

7

Valuation Fits Risk/Reward

Valuation is critical. A valuation that is too high is often the death knell of a potential investment.
