Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

Driving innovation and economic growth in Central Illinois through strategic angel investment.

Our Vision

Central Illinois Angels seeks to be the primary tool for entrepreneurs looking for equity and strategic advice to grow their business exponentially.

Our Mission

Central Illinois Angels will assist early stage companies by providing investment capital, contacts, strategic advice, and mentoring. We will work with promising startups to provide expertise and assistance to facilitate regional economic growth.

We accomplish this through utilizing the combined industry and related expertise of the members of the angel investment group. We also utilize a structured deal flow process that begins with screening the company, continues with the due diligence process, and is maintained in working with the company after an investment has been made.

We strive to generate value for the companies we invest in, for our members, and for the local economy through mentoring and expertise.

How We Create Value

Strategic Investment

We provide not just capital, but strategic advice and mentoring to help companies grow exponentially.

Expert Guidance

Leveraging the combined industry expertise of our members to facilitate regional economic growth.

Structured Process

A proven deal flow process from screening through due diligence and ongoing company support.

Value Creation

Generating value for portfolio companies, our members, and the local economy through active engagement.
