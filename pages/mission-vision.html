About Us

Our Mission & Vision

Driven by a commitment to foster innovation and economic growth in Central Illinois.

👁
Our Vision

A Thriving Innovation Ecosystem

We envision Central Illinois as a nationally recognized hub for entrepreneurship and innovation, where talented founders have access to the capital, mentorship, and networks they need to build world-class companies.

By investing in the next generation of entrepreneurs, we aim to create lasting economic impact, high-quality jobs, and a vibrant community that attracts and retains top talent.

Our Mission

Empowering Entrepreneurs, Enriching Communities

Central Illinois Angels exists to provide early-stage capital and strategic support to promising companies in the Central Illinois region. We bring together accredited investors who share a passion for entrepreneurship and community development.

Our mission extends beyond financial returns. We are committed to mentoring founders, sharing our expertise, and building an ecosystem where innovation thrives and local businesses succeed on a global stage.

🎯
Our Impact

How We Create Value

We go beyond writing checks. Our approach combines capital with hands-on support.

💰

Strategic Investment

We provide flexible capital structures tailored to each company's needs, from convertible notes to equity rounds, with fair terms that align interests.

👥

Active Mentorship

Our members serve as advisors and board members, offering guidance on strategy, operations, hiring, and growth based on decades of experience.

🔗

Network Access

We open doors to customers, partners, talent, and follow-on investors through our extensive professional network across Central Illinois and beyond.

📈

Market Expertise

Our investors bring deep domain knowledge across technology, healthcare, agriculture, and manufacturing to help companies navigate their markets.

🎓

Educational Programs

We host workshops, speaker events, and pitch nights that help entrepreneurs develop their skills and connect with the broader startup community.

🌎

Community Impact

Every investment we make strengthens the local economy, creates jobs, and contributes to making Central Illinois a better place to live and work.
