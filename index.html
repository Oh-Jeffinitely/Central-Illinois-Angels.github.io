Who We Are

Our Mission

Central Illinois Angels is a group of accredited investors dedicated to providing early-stage capital, mentorship, and strategic guidance to promising startups in the Central Illinois region.

We believe in the potential of local entrepreneurs and are committed to fostering innovation that drives economic growth in our community.
